About the Project

Cyber Risk Map - Hail Municipality

Overview

The Cyber Risk Map is an interactive platform designed to visualize the dependencies between systems at Hail Municipality. It helps the cybersecurity department understand how a failure or breach in one system can affect others, enabling proactive risk management and informed decision-making.

Problem

Municipalities operate multiple interconnected systems, but there is no clear tool that shows how these systems depend on each other. When an incident occurs, it's hard to quickly identify which systems are affected and what the overall impact will be.

Solution

  • Asset Inventory: A complete list of systems with their security status.
  • Dependency Map: A visual graph showing how systems depend on each other.
  • What-If Simulation: A tool to simulate failures and see the impact.
  • AI Reports: Automated risk assessment and recommendations.

Methodology

The project follows the standards and guidelines of the National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA), specifically the Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC). The risk assessment model evaluates each system based on threats, vulnerabilities, existing controls, dependencies, and criticality to business operations.
